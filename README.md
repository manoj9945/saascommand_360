\# SaaSCommand 360



SaaSCommand 360 is a SaaS product, revenue, and customer-success analytics platform.



The project combines product usage, subscriptions, billing, and support data to provide a centralized view of customer health, revenue, product adoption, and business actions.



\## Project Objectives



\- Monitor customer health and product usage

\- Analyze SaaS revenue and subscriptions

\- Track product adoption and engagement

\- Monitor billing and payment-related events

\- Analyze customer support performance

\- Identify churn-risk and expansion opportunities

\- Generate automated business alerts and actions

\- Provide dashboards and APIs for business users
